## The task

Do a complete read-through of every piece of copy on the site
in both capital and operator modes. Audit against these criteria:

1. Does the capital side speak clearly to a founder raising money?
   They should feel like WND understands their world,
   moves fast, and has genuine skin in the game.

2. Does the operator side speak clearly to a company that needs
   a senior operator partner?
   They should feel like WND has done the work,
   not just advised on it.

3. Is every stat correctly labeled?
   - $340M = R&D directed across major businesses. Not AUM.
   - $2B = revenue navigated as operators. Not exits.
   - $1.5B = WMS exit to Scientific Games, October 2013. Verified.
   - $400M+ = products launched concept to scale.
   Do NOT change these numbers. Only change labels if they are wrong.

4. Is the voice consistent? Human, specific, story-driven.
   Not boastful. Not resume-like. Not corporate.
   Facts do the boasting. Larry does not claim, he shows.

5. Are there any em-dashes anywhere? Remove every one.
   Replace with a comma, a period, or rewrite the sentence.

6. Is anything invented, assumed, or unverifiable?
   Flag it. Do not include unverifiable claims.
